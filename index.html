About me

Hi, I'm Shashank - a Data Engineer at PwC and an adventure lover staying in Bengaluru. With a Master's in Data Analytics from NIT Trichy, my work revolves around building robust data solutions, specializing in large-scale optimization and architectural design using SQL, PySpark, and Databricks.

Beyond the screen, I am drawn to the great outdoors. Whether I am trekking to explore new destinations, keeping up my fitness with a run through Cubbon Park, or capturing landscapes through my camera lens, I'm always on the move.

I am currently open to freelance opportunities that challenge me to build efficient data pipelines. If you are looking to collaborate, fill this form to get started! Otherwise, hit me up if you want to chat about tech or your favorite trekking trail :D
